A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT BE WONDERING

Good questions.
Clear answers.

What is this website?+

An independently built landing page inspired by Parakeet AI. It demonstrates an interview assistant concept and is not the official Parakeet AI service.

Does the demo use my microphone?+

No. It uses predefined sample questions and answers. No microphone, camera, or interview content is accessed or stored.

Can I use it in a live interview?+

The interview workspace supports typed questions, microphone dictation, and meeting-tab audio transcription. AI answers require server configuration. Open the assistant from the navigation. Always follow your interviewer’s rules about assistance.

Do I need to install anything?+

No. Explore the page and interactive demo directly in your browser, on mobile or desktop.
